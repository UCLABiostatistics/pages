# UCLA Biostatistics
## News and Resources

A collection of newsletters, departmental updates, and shared materials for the UCLA Biostatistics community, including faculty, students, staff, alumni, and collaborators.

### Explore

- **Newsletters** — Current and past editions of the department newsletter, featuring research, achievements, student activities, and community news.
- **Events and seminars** — Announcements, programs, presentation slides, and materials from departmental events.
- **Teaching and research resources** — Educational materials, computational tools, and useful references.
- **Community resources** — Information about opportunities, professional development, and other topics of interest to our department.
